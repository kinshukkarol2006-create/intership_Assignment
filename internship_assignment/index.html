Advancing the Future of Digital Risk

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) trains and deploys the next generation of digital, cyber and AI risk professionals through academic collaboration and real-world industry practice.

About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on digital, cyber and technology risk roles in regulated, high-consequence environments.

Through partnerships with universities and industry organisations, IDR bridges the gap between academic insight and real-world implementation.

Our Model

Academy

Training programs and bootcamps designed to develop digital risk, cybersecurity and AI governance professionals.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing future digital risk models, governance frameworks and technology risk research.

Advisory Services

Helping organisations implement frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2.

Community

IDR supports students, early-career professionals and experienced practitioners across industries including financial services, technology and critical infrastructure seeking expertise in digital and cyber risk.
